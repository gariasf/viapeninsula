# Traced shapes are stored as the pieces of track they share, and slots as chains

ADR-0014 split the bundle by region and kept long distance out until its shapes came in shared pieces (`docs/research/bundle-at-scale.md`: Renfe's long-distance Trains run the same rails again and again, so most of a traced shape's points are another's). #257 is that, gated at its triage on 4 October 2026 on #259's dry run: under 60% smaller, stop. On the dry run of 10 October 2026 (Renfe's long-distance file of 9 October, as #259 traces it) long distance's 636 runs have 1,323,157 points, 131,790 of them distinct, and 122,922 slots; stored as pieces and chains they are 81.0% smaller, brotli (598.9 KB for 3,151.4 KB, the rows below added up).

- **A piece is a run of points between junctions, shape ends and turn-backs,** found over a region's traced shapes together: where a shape starts or ends, where it turns back on its own track, and where the shapes' tracks don't just carry on, a point with other than two points next to it among all of them. Each piece is stored once, as its first point in millionths of a degree and then, for each point after, the change in the step to it from the step before, as a straight track's are nearly nothing. A shape is a list of signed piece numbers, negative where it runs a piece the other way. A point is its coordinates, so a shape and its `:back` are the same pieces, and so are two runs that took the same track of OpenStreetMap, whichever Line they were traced for.
- **A shape's distances aren't stored.** The page sums the great-circle lengths between its points and rounds, as the build makes `dist`, and the file lists where that is a metre off, which is where the build summed points it has since rounded to 6 decimals: 6.0% of long distance's points, never more than a metre, 38 KB. A shape that doesn't start at 0, as one cut at the border keeps counting from where it began, gives its distances whole where that is shorter than its fixes. The page's engine and the build's needn't round the last bit of `Math.sin`, `cos` and `asin` alike (macOS's JavaScriptCore and V8 differ in 3.3% of long distance's running sums, by up to 1.2e-10 m): no rounded distance differs, as the closest a sum comes to half a metre is 3.4e-6 m.
- **A shape's levels are the vertices they start at,** with the level's name from a table: 58 KB of long distance's shapes' levels are 9 KB. A level that starts between two vertices stays as it is.
- **A shape's slots in one graph and band are a chain.** Each runs on from where the one before ended, on every shape of every region built on 10 October 2026, so a slot is its length, its side, which centreline it goes on (as the change in its place in a table), where on it it starts (as the change from where the one before ended) and how far it goes; a chain's first slot starts where its shape's does, which is 0 but for a shape cut at the border. All or nothing: if one slot of a track doesn't follow the one before on its shape, or names a Line or shape the track hasn't, every slot of the track stays whole, as they were, and the build says so (`trackFile()`).
- **Centrelines, smoothed copies and curves stay as they were,** the shapes whose IDs start with `stretch:`. They, the strokes and the rails are what's left of long distance's 2.8 MB (#262 will draw it): 1.8 MB of it is the centrelines, smoothed copies and curves.
- **The page, the recorder and the measures read a track file back as the track the build made** (`decodeTrack()`), so the engine and the web code see the `Track` they always did; the page decodes each file once. A file of a build before this, with its shapes and slots whole, is read as it is.
- **The build checks what it writes.** `trackFile()` reads the file back as JSON has it and compares it to the track it made; where they differ the region's build fails and keeps its last files (ADR-0014), rather than publish a track that isn't the one built.

## Considered Options

- **Each piece's lengths stored, as #257's wording has it.** Rejected: a shape's distances round a running sum, so one piece has different whole-metre lengths in the shapes that reach it at a different fraction of a metre: 44% of the pieces that two or more of long distance's shapes run, and 25% of its steps are a metre off the rounded length between their two points. Rounding each step's length instead moves a 700 km shape's distances by up to 51 m, and with them every Trip's calls and every stroke's `from` and `to`. Lengths in millimetres cost more than the points do.
- **Slots per Line and piece, as #257's wording has it.** Rejected: a slot is one line fitted to the points of a run of a shape on one centreline, from where that run starts, along the shape's rounded distances, so two shapes over one piece have the same points but not always the same slots: 66.1% of long distance's slots repeat an earlier one of their Line's and band exactly, 34% don't, and of the pairs of a Line and a piece that two or more of its shapes run, they disagree over 16%, which carry 58% of the slots. One table entry for each Line and piece can't be exact, then. One with a variant for each way the shapes differ can, and costs more: tried on long distance's 122,922 slots, read back exactly, it's 166.6 KB brotli beside the chains' 133.9 KB, 147.1 KB with each piece's first slot coded against an anchor, the centreline and place its piece starts at, that the Lines share (Catalonia's 31.2 beside 26.9); a dictionary of the kinds of slot for each Line and band, the repeats shared, 174.9 KB. brotli already finds the repeats along a Line's chains.
- **Points kept at OpenStreetMap's 7 decimals,** so that distances come out exactly with no fixes. Rejected: it carries the unrounded points through every `Shape`, costs a digit for each coordinate, and a Station's point on a way is on no grid.
- **Each number as a base-64 string,** and **a piece's start as a change from the last piece's end.** Rejected: 2.5% and 2% of the pieces for more code and, for the first, a string to parse in the page.
- **A binary encoding of the whole file** (`pbf` is a dependency). Not now: it needs a decoder for the strokes and centrelines too, which are most of what's left.

## Consequences

- **Long distance's traced shapes and slots,** brotli: shapes 2,624.1 KB to 465.0 KB, slots 527.3 KB to 133.9 KB, together 3,151.4 KB to 598.9 KB; raw 57,760 KB to 4,376 KB. Its region's whole track file, without Stations, is 5,334 KB brotli and would be 2,771 KB, 48.1% smaller (#262).
- **Today's 15 regions' track files** come to 1,048.6 KB brotli from 1,460.4 KB, 28.2% smaller; traced shapes and slots together 714.7 KB to 289.7 KB. Catalonia's is 614.7 KB to 438.0 KB. #257's triage had measured 13%.
- **The page's cost.** In the page's own path (headless Chromium, `decodeTrack()` from the dev server, median of 7) Catalonia's file parses in 6.7 ms and decodes in 5.3 where parsing the old one took 11.3, and with the CPU slowed 4× in 22.8 and 13.8 where it took 43.6, holding 9.2 MB of heap where the old one held 10.8. In Node, cold, a process for each of 5 runs: 23 to 25 ms (11 and 13) where parsing the old one took 24 to 27, in 14.8 MB where it took 17.9; long distance's in 170 to 180 ms (77 and 95) where it was 250 to 263, in 129 MB where it was 200, as the points in a piece are one array for every shape that runs it.
- **A page built before this can't use a track file of this form.** Main's page code on these files, measured on 10 October 2026 (3b0317d, headless Chromium): a tab already open, on main's bundle with 318 Trains, keeps its Lines and Stations at its next look at the manifest, within a minute, and loses every Train (a `TypeError` in `show()`, which finds no slots), and a page opened on them draws no Lines, no Stations and no Trains, until it's reloaded. That is worse for an open tab than #256's split, whose old page kept what it had. The daily build and the deploy run back to back, as for #28's and #256's splits (#256's were 38 s apart), and a reload fixes it. No version handling is built. A page built after this reads a file of a build before as it is, as yesterday's track is in the manifest for a day after a deploy.
- A shape or slot a later change makes `encodeTrack()` unable to give back fails its region's build, not the map.
