# Traced shapes are stored as the pieces of track they share, and slots as chains

ADR-0014 split the bundle by region and kept long distance out until its shapes came in shared pieces (`docs/research/bundle-at-scale.md`: Renfe's long-distance Trains run the same rails again and again, so most of a traced shape's points are another's). #257 is that, gated at its triage on 4 October 2026 on #259's dry run: under 60% smaller, stop. On the dry run of 10 October 2026 (Renfe's long-distance file of 9 October, as #259 traces it) long distance's 636 runs have 1,323,157 points, 131,790 of them distinct, and 122,922 slots; stored as pieces and chains they are 81.0% smaller, brotli (599.9 KB for 3,154.0 KB).

- **A piece is a run of points between junctions, shape ends and turn-backs,** found over a region's traced shapes together: where a shape starts or ends, where it turns back on its own track, and where the shapes' tracks don't just carry on, a point with other than two points next to it among all of them. Each piece is stored once, as its first point in millionths of a degree and then, for each point after, the change in the step to it from the step before, as a straight track's are nearly nothing. A shape is a list of signed piece numbers, negative where it runs a piece the other way. A point is its coordinates, so a shape and its `:back` are the same pieces, and so are two runs that took the same track of OpenStreetMap, whichever Line they were traced for.
- **A shape's distances aren't stored.** The page sums the great-circle lengths between its points and rounds, as the build makes `dist`, and the file lists where that is a metre off, which is where the build summed points it has since rounded to 6 decimals: 6.0% of long distance's points, never more than a metre, 38 KB. A shape that doesn't start at 0, as one cut at the border keeps counting from where it began, gives its distances whole where that is shorter than its fixes.
- **A shape's levels are the vertices they start at,** with the level's name from a table: 58 KB of long distance's shapes' levels are 9 KB. A level that starts between two vertices stays as it is.
- **A shape's slots in one graph and band are a chain.** Each runs on from where the one before ended, on every shape of every region built on 10 October 2026, so a slot is its length, its side, which centreline it goes on (as the change in its place in a table), where on it it starts (as the change from where the one before ended) and how far it goes; only the first starts where the shape's first slot does, which is 0 but for a shape cut at the border. Slots that aren't chains, or that name a Line or shape the track hasn't, stay whole.
- **Centrelines, smoothed copies and curves stay as they were,** the shapes whose IDs start with `stretch:`. They, the strokes and the rails are what's left of long distance's 2.8 MB (#262 will draw it): 1.8 MB of it is the centrelines, smoothed copies and curves.
- **The page, the recorder and the measures read a track file back as the track the build made** (`decodeTrack()`), so the engine and the web code see the `Track` they always did; the page decodes each file once. A file of a build before this, with its shapes and slots whole, is read as it is.
- **The build checks what it writes.** `trackFile()` reads the file back as JSON has it and compares it to the track it made; where they differ the region's build fails and keeps its last files (ADR-0014), rather than publish a track that isn't the one built.

## Considered Options

- **Each piece's lengths stored, as #257's wording has it.** Rejected: a shape's distances round a running sum, so one piece has different whole-metre lengths in the shapes that reach it at a different fraction of a metre: 25% of long distance's steps are a metre off the rounded length of their segment. Rounding each segment instead moves a 700 km shape's distances by up to 51 m, and with them every Trip's calls and every stroke's `from` and `to`. Lengths in millimetres cost more than the points do.
- **Slots per Line and piece, as #257's wording has it.** Rejected: a slot is one line fitted to the points of a run of a shape on one centreline, and the fit starts where that run does, so two shapes over one piece have the same points but not the same slots (66% of long distance's slots repeat an earlier one of their Line's exactly, 34% don't); a table of piece by piece would change today's slots, or need a correction at each boundary. A dictionary of the slots that do repeat, kept for each Line and band, costs 168 KB beside the chains' 134 KB for the same slots, as brotli already finds the repeats along a Line's chains.
- **Points kept at OpenStreetMap's 7 decimals,** so that distances come out exactly with no fixes. Rejected: it carries the unrounded points through every `Shape`, costs a digit for each coordinate, and a Station's point on a way is on no grid.
- **Each number as a base-64 string,** and **a piece's start as a change from the last piece's end.** Rejected: 2.5% and 2% of the pieces for more code and, for the first, a string to parse in the page.
- **A binary encoding of the whole file** (`pbf` is a dependency). Not now: it needs a decoder for the strokes and centrelines too, which are most of what's left.

## Consequences

- **Long distance's traced shapes and slots,** brotli: shapes 2,624.1 KB to 465.0 KB, slots 527.3 KB to 133.9 KB, together 3,154.0 KB to 599.9 KB; raw 57,760 KB to 4,377 KB. Its region's whole track file, without Stations, is 5,334 KB brotli and would be 2,771 KB, 48.1% smaller (#262).
- **Today's 15 regions' track files** come to 1,046.9 KB brotli from 1,460.1 KB, 28.3% smaller; traced shapes and slots together 716.6 KB to 289.3 KB. Catalonia's is 614.7 KB to 438.0 KB. #257's triage had measured 13%.
- **The page's cost,** in Node: Catalonia's track parses and decodes in 32 to 42 ms from cold where parsing the old one took 31 to 34, in 26 MB of heap where it took 32; long distance's in 141 ms where it was 206, in 163 MB where it was 286, as the points in a piece are one array for every shape that runs it.
- A track file of this form isn't one a page built before this can use: it finds no shape for a Trip, and draws no Trains from its next look at the manifest until it's reloaded. The daily build and the deploy run back to back, as for #28's and #256's splits. A page built after this reads yesterday's track in the manifest as it is, for the hours until a build drops it.
- A shape or slot a later change makes `encodeTrack()` unable to give back fails its region's build, not the map.
