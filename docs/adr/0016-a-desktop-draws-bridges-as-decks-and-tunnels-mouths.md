# A desktop draws bridges as decks and tunnels' mouths

#213 asked whether 3D was worth its weight for this map, and where heights would come from. Its look (22 Oct 2026, [the sheet](https://github.com/gariasf/viapeninsula/issues/213#issuecomment-6347517914)) drew, tilted 45°, the ground from a DEM (G), the track's bridges as decks and its tunnels' mouths from the levels the trace already keeps (L, ADR-0015's `levels`), both (GL), GL with the Lines in tunnels hidden (GLh), and skipped Units as blocks (U). The maintainer picked L, as the look built it, for a desktop ([the picks](https://github.com/gariasf/viapeninsula/issues/213#issuecomment-6347835317)), but for the look's shortcut: its deck was a grey slab floating over each track, the Line left on the ground under it. Built in #213, 22 Oct 2026:

- **Bridges as decks, from zoom 14** (`DECKS_FROM`). On each bridge of 150 m or more (the trace's BRIEF merges shorter ones into the ground, ADR-0006), each Line that crosses it goes up onto it: its stroke there is drawn as a deck, a `fill-extrusion` in its own colour, side by side as its stroke is, at the Lines' offsets in the band; from zoom 15, where the Lines are back on their rails, on its rails, grey where Lines share them (#139), once for each track. Its flat stroke isn't drawn under it from zoom 14: below, the Line goes over the bridge on the ground, as ever.
  - **Its height is set on the screen:** 12 px over the ground tilted 45° on a bridge of OpenStreetMap's layer 1, and 6 px more for each layer above, every bridge of a layer alike, as `layer` orders bridges and doesn't measure them; a bridge with no layer or layer 0 stands as one of layer 1. In metres at zoom 14 at the bridge's latitude, halved for each zoom in by an exponential curve of base ½, which MapLibre interpolates between two zooms exactly, so it stands as high at every zoom. Its slab is 2 px, as high.
  - **It ramps down to the ground** at each end over 80 m, two piers, in 10 steps, or a third of a bridge shorter than 240 m; but where it meets another deck of its Line's, as at a centreline's end, it carries on.
  - **It stands on a pier every 40 m**, 3 m square, in a neutral grey, as high as the deck there.
  - **Along the Stretches, a deck is made for each quarter zoom** from 14 to 15 (`DECK_STEPS`), each a layer of its own: over that zoom the Lines move onto their rails (APART goes from a line width to none), and a deck is fixed in metres, so made once it would drift up to 8 px off its stroke for each line width the stroke is off its centreline; made each quarter zoom, at most 1.8 px.
  - **Decks that overlap are lifted a hair apart,** at most 2.3 m at zoom 14, under half a pixel tilted, so that they don't flicker, and the one on top is the one whose stroke is: two regions' on the track they share, drawn over each other (ADR-0014), by tiers as their strokes, Media Distancia y Avant's under long distance's under the region's own; and one region's, as a double track's two on their rails, Lines' on a crowded Stretch, and any two as the Lines move onto their rails, by where each lies across its way, so that two 1.5 to 12 m apart differ.
- **Tunnels' mouths, from zoom 13:** an arch at each, standing up to the viewer, once: where a traced shape goes under the ground or comes out of it, as a Line goes `under` (bundle's `depth()`), not where it starts or ends in its tunnel, and those of tracks within 25 m of each other one, as a double track's two tracks, mapped apart, have one mouth; and where regions' tracks go into one tunnel, as long distance's, Media Distancia y Avant's and Catalonia's do through Barcelona, the first region's in the manifest's order.
- **Tunnels are drawn as ever** (`under`, `covered`), and the Trains in them. No ground, no DEM, and no new credits.
- **Flat or tilted, alike.** A desktop draws the decks at any pitch: flat, from straight above, a deck is its Line's stroke, a little raised.
- **Only a desktop,** which the page lays out with the tabs as a rail, `(min-width: 640px) and (min-height: 480px)`, read once at load: not `wide`, as a phone turned sideways is wide but keeps the tabs' bar. A phone keeps the flat map and #326's tilt, fetches no decks file and loads no decks code, until milestone 9's Order 4, after #853.
- **The file layout: a decks file for each region,** only a desktop's, beside its track file and named after it, `days/decks-<region>-<the track file's hash>.json`, `{ "bridges": { "<centreline>": [[from, to, layer?], …] } }`: where each centreline of the one line graph from zoom 10 up runs on a bridge, from where to where along it in whole metres, and the bridge's layer where it's above 1, by the levels of the pieces its Stretches are drawn along, as a stroke goes `under` in a tunnel by them. Only the strokes along the Stretches need it, as only the build has their pieces' levels: the page cuts them at those runs, and each part on a bridge is its deck. The rails' bridges and the mouths the page finds from the traced shapes' levels, which the track file carries already. The track and Trips files, and the manifest, are the same as before, byte for byte. Named after the track file, the manifest needs no name for it, so a phone reads no more; published for a day (`max-age=86400`), not for good, as a later build of the same track may cut its bridges by another rule under the same name.
- **The decks are made in the tiles they're in.** At a region's join, the Lines' worker cuts its strokes and rails at their bridges and keeps each deck's line, 2,780 of long distance's on 22 Oct 2026's dry run; a tile of the third source, `decks`, from zoom 14 makes the decks in it for its zoom, each quarter zoom's below 15 and the rails' from there. Made all at the join, long distance's would be some 276,000 features, 440 ms to make in Node and 201 ms more to index, where a view shows a few hundred: kept as lines, its join takes 164 ms in Node where main's takes 99, Media Distancia y Avant's 126 where 91, Catalonia's 39 as main's, medians of 5; and a tile takes at most 6 ms at zoom 14 for long distance, 10 ms for Catalonia, and under 1 ms from 15.
- **A desktop begins its decks the first time its map reaches zoom 13,** the mouths' zoom, in an idle callback (`decksGate()`), and keeps them from then. Until then, it asks for no decks file, its Lines' worker cuts no stroke at a bridge, and the decks' layers aren't added: no deck is drawn further out. Then it asks for the decks files of the tracks it has, and the worker makes their Lines again with them; a later look's tracks come with theirs. Handed at every join, on 22 Oct 2026's 08:30 capture, the decks took S3's longest frame, where the page lays out the Lines of eight Cercanías regions joining at zoom 6, from 241–268 ms on main to 283–292 (#213's box 5), with no decks code in the frame; with them off, it took 242–250.
- **The code a desktop alone runs is in two chunks a phone never fetches:** the Lines' worker's `decks.ts`, which it loads the first time a region comes with its decks file, and which, where it fails to load, leaves the Lines on the ground, as a phone's; and the page's `deckLayers.ts`, its layers, which imports nothing the page's own code does, so that the page a phone fetches is one file still. For that, the workers are built as modules (`worker.format: 'es'`), as main.ts makes them, and the page preloads no module it imports where it needs it (`build.modulePreload: false`).

## Considered Options

From the sheet, tilted 45°, on 22 Oct 2026's bundle at its 08:30 capture, medians of 3, against main tilted (A):

- **The ground (G),** Mapterhorn's DEM, keyless, CNIG's 2 m model under CC BY 4.0, exaggerated 1.5×, with a hillshade. Rejected: it loads 0.55–1.1 MB before the first Lines (17–60% more), tiles as the view moves, logs Mapterhorn's 404s off the coast, and adds two lines of credits; it drapes the viaduct's Line 77 m (×1.5) into its valley and R2 Sud over Garraf's cliffs, combs the sea's edge, and barely reads in the cities' cores at zoom 13, where the map is most used. Mapterhorn stays the source to try if the ground comes back, credited as the sheet words it.
- **The ground and decks (GL).** Rejected: G's costs, and decks spanned from the ground wait for its tiles and jump, step where it changes fast, lie flat on flat ground (Bellvitge's 1,165 m bridge), and the Line still drapes under its own deck; on the desktop, S8 ran 17 ms/s and 6 fps worse, beyond the runs' spread.
- **GL with the Lines in tunnels hidden (GLh).** Rejected: it empties Barcelona's core, the Metro, Rodalies through the centre and most of FGC, the Trains' pills left on Lines that aren't drawn.
- **Mouths only.** Rejected at the picks: it costs no more than L, and shows no bridge.
- **Units as blocks (U).** Not built: each block bends along its track as its Train moves, so it's geometry every frame, a second GeoJSON source set on every frame of a follow, as MapLibre 6.11 has no model or instancing layer; the bundle knows no Units' lengths; and S8 already breaches its budget, main tilted at 978 ms/s against ≤ 400 on the desktop.
- **A grey deck over a flat Line,** the look's L. Rejected at the picks: the Line is left on the ground under it, the deck floats 12 px over it from end to end, starting and ending in the air, and below zoom 15 it's off the Lines, as it follows the rails; on shared track it's grey, as the viaduct's six Lines share it.
- **Cutting the decks in the page, from the rails' levels,** as the look did. Rejected: they follow the rails, not the Lines' strokes, and it cost long distance's worker 164 ms and Media Distancia y Avant's 101 ms (Node) at their joins. **Mapping the rails' bridges onto the strokes by the slots,** which the track file has, would need no file, but puts a bridge's ends up to 25 m off (ALONG), where the build has its pieces' levels.
- **A deck made once along the Stretches,** at zoom 14. Rejected: as the Lines move onto their rails from 14 to 15 it drifts off its stroke, by up to 8 px for each line width the stroke is off its centreline, 20 px for the outermost of six side by side; a quarter zoom's steps keep it within 1.8 px for each. **All its features made at the join:** above.
- **A deck's height in metres,** 25 m on layer 1 and 10 m a layer. Rejected in the look: 5 px at zoom 14, read as a line beside the Line, and 40 px at zoom 17. **From the ground,** as GL spans them: needs the ground.
- **The decks file named in the manifest,** by its own content, as the track's is. Rejected: on 22 Oct 2026's manifest, a name for each region's day took it from 2,042 B to 2,367 B brotli, which every page, a phone's too, reads every minute.
- **The bridges in the track file,** as the strokes cut there, or the centrelines' levels. Rejected: a phone loads the track file.
- **The decks' code in the page's file and the worker's,** as their own modules need the workers built as modules and no preload. Rejected: a phone would load some 3 KB more of it, brotli, the two chunks' 3,122 B.

## Consequences

- **The decks files,** on 22 Oct 2026's dry run, which every other file of matches main's byte for byte: 8,877 B brotli in all, 27,413 B raw; long distance's 3,579 B, Media Distancia y Avant's 3,533 B, Catalonia's 486 B and each other region's 158 B or less, 17 B where it has no bridge, against 2.68 MB brotli of the 18 track files. On it, 1,321 bridges on the centrelines: long distance's 614, 311 km, Media Distancia y Avant's 583, 270 km, Catalonia's 62, 22 km; and 3,199 mouths, each region's once, which are 2,062 once where regions' tracks go into one tunnel.
- **On a phone,** by `vite build`s of 23 Oct 2026 against main's, the page's file is 633 B larger brotli and the Lines' worker's 594 B, for the few lines that ask for the decks and load their chunks, and the worker's built as a module with a chunk to load; MapLibre's worker's 97 B and the names' 2 B, built as modules: 1,326 B in all, and no more data. A desktop loads the two chunks, 3,122 B (`decks.js` 2,445 B, `deckLayers.js` 677 B), and its regions' decks files.
- **A Train on a bridge is drawn on the ground,** under its deck, as before. Raising it takes more than a style property: MapLibre's `symbol-height-offset` (from 6.6, data-driven, in metres) could lift its pill and its count, but its dot and the rings round a followed Train are `circle` layers, which have no height, so they'd be drawn as symbols; and the page would need each Train's height where it is, on its bridge's layer, up a ramp, and from zoom 15 on its rails' deck, which only the Lines' worker's decks know, each time a Train moves.
- **What the map draws over a Line's stroke is drawn on the ground on a bridge:** the glow under the Line whose screen it is (#473), and a Closure's coats (#341).
- **A curve across a node on a bridge stays on the ground,** and the decks either side of it ramp down to it: on 22 Oct 2026's dry run, 158 of the 6,010 ends of the decks along the Stretches, 2.6%, ramp down within 60 m of another of their Line's. Long distance draws some of its Lines on both ways' centrelines over a high-speed line's bridges, as OUIGO at side 0.5 on one and −0.5 on the other, so their decks lie on each other, in one colour, as their strokes do.
- **A track built before #213 has no decks file,** as a region's whose build fails keeps its last build's: a desktop's request for it fails, a 404 in the console each time the map looks for its days anew, and its region's Lines stay on the ground until the region builds again.
- **Perf** is gated on `npm run perf -- S2 S3 S4 S5bcn S8`, tilted (`--pitch 45`), on a desktop and a phone, against main (#213's box 5).
