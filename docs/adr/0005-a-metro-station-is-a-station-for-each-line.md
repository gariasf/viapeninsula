# A metro station is a Station for each Line calling there

TMB publishes a metro station served by several Lines as a stop for each Line, grouped under a parent station whose point sits at one Line's platforms: at Passeig de Gràcia it's 270 m from L3's. Tracing a Line through its Stations (ADR-0004) and placing its Trips on its track need each Station on that Line's own rails, so each Line's stop is a Station of its own, known by TMB's ID for it: `tmb:1.327` is L3 at Passeig de Gràcia. FGC's and TRAM's feeds make each platform a stop of its own too, but their parent stations sit on the platforms, so there the parent station is the Station.

## Considered Options

- **TMB's parent stations as Stations**, one for each place, as people know them. Rejected: the parent's point lies off some of its Lines' rails, too far for those Lines to be traced through it.

## Consequences

- A metro station served by several Lines is that many Stations with the same name, up to a few hundred metres apart: two Sants Estació (L3 and L5), three Passeig de Gràcia (L2, L3 and L4). Each keeps the parent station TMB groups it in as its place, at the middle of its Lines' Stations rather than at the parent's own point, which lies off some Lines' rails (#27). The map names each place once, beside the Metro's track nearest it (#120, #143), but draws each of its Stations on its own Lines: on the bands, a dot in the middle of its own Lines' strokes, and from zoom 15 its own mark on its own track, linked to its place's others. From zoom 13 to 15, the place is one mark, its Network's, at its middle (#210, built by #572). Tapping any of them opens one Station board listing every one of its Lines' departures (#17); a railway Station beside it is a place of its own, with a board of its own, unless the two make an interchange (below).
- Anything that names a Station, such as a share link (#19) or the Metro's live data (#12), names one Line's stop. A share link opens the board of that Station's place, or of its interchange (#210, built by #572).
- Two Networks' Stations are one place the same way where an operator publishes its Station at another Network's point, as Renfe publishes Martorell Central at FGC's, 60 m from R4: the build moves it onto its own track, where its Trains stop, and the two share a place, drawn between the tracks (#158).
- Places of other Networks that people change between on foot, as Passeig de Gràcia's Rodalies Station and its Metro place, make an interchange. From zoom 13 it's drawn as one badge of its Networks' marks, named once, and its places share one board, as Martorell Central's two Networks share a place: a tap on the badge or on any of its places, or a link to any of its Stations, opens it, with all its places' departures. So where a railway Station beside a metro station makes an interchange with it, it's still a place of its own, but the two share one board (#210, built by #572). Which places make one is #572's to decide.
