# How railisland puts Trains on their own track

Research for #68: how railisland (軌島, [railisland.tw](https://railisland.tw), source [siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live)) draws each Train on its own track, zoomed out and zoomed in; how it draws Train models and what they cost on a phone; and which of its ideas fit our v1 map. Its licence is source-available ([ADR-0001](../adr/0001-own-code-railisland-is-study-only.md)), so this note describes its mechanisms in our own words and copies no code.

- **Date:** 2026-09-26, a Saturday. Code read at commit [`65a7adc`](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/commit/65a7adc729e7267f7fdc1ee901346a5598cb682c) (21:14 +08:00). The live site was serving build v0926l, commit [`f5b54fd`](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/commit/f5b54fd8f8b22c129ec4d6530055e97463ced715) from 16:14 the same day. Between the two only the train garage and the changelog changed, and the ten served 3D scripts compared were byte-identical to the repo's. Browser measurements ran 21:47–22:04 Taipei time.
- **Method:** read the [README](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/blob/65a7adc729e7267f7fdc1ee901346a5598cb682c/README.md), [`rail-3d/README.md`](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/blob/65a7adc729e7267f7fdc1ee901346a5598cb682c/rail-3d/README.md), [`rail-3d/physical/README.md`](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/blob/65a7adc729e7267f7fdc1ee901346a5598cb682c/rail-3d/physical/README.md), [`FORMATIONS.md`](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/blob/65a7adc729e7267f7fdc1ee901346a5598cb682c/rail-3d/integration/FORMATIONS.md), the map code in `index.html` and the `rail-3d/` modules; fetched the served assets and data files; loaded the live site in Chromium through Playwright for load size and frame rates (see Measured). Zooms here are MapLibre's, as ours are; railisland's own code counts one higher ([`index.html` L7584–L7588](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/blob/65a7adc729e7267f7fdc1ee901346a5598cb682c/index.html#L7584-L7588)). ## TL;DR

- **Separate geometry per track, no offset maths.** railisland keeps OpenStreetMap's tracks as mapped, and an offline solver gives each TRA (Taiwan Railway), high-speed (HSR) and Alishan Train a path through them, platform tracks and passing loops included. Those Trains sit on their paths at every zoom. Metros get one track per direction, in the 3D view only.
- **A hard switch at zoom 14.** Below it: one schematic stroke per Line in MapLibre `line` layers, and Trains painted on a 2D canvas over the map. Both ways of a double track share a pixel there, and the only markers moved aside are TRA Trains close together going the same way, such as one being overtaken at a Station. From 14 the strokes are filtered out and a three.js layer draws every track, the Train models, viaducts and portals.
- **Models:** three.js r170 in MapLibre custom layers; no deck.gl, no glTF, and no fill-extrusion for Trains. 62 Blender-made models ship as raw vertex buffers, one mesh per car, each car placed at its own distance along the track. The glow is soft ellipses per car, and every 2D marker gets a soft halo too; underground track and Trains are drawn faint in a see-through pass.
- **Cost:** a cold load moved 15.5 MB, 11.3 MB of it track and height data fetched even if you never zoom in. Zooming into Taipei Main Station added 5.8 MB of car meshes. With the CPU slowed 4×, frames fell from 37 fps zoomed out to about 17 fps there, and turning the models off barely helped.
- **Markers and Lines, a second look (2026-09-27):** zoomed in, railisland's Trains are pills with their Train number or Line code, shaped and filled by kind of service, with a small arrow outside the leading edge. Its Lines have a casing, and its Stations five sizes. #90 takes the pills, with our Line names, the kind of service in the shape and Live or Scheduled in the fill (section 4).
- **For our v1:** our per-direction tracing ([ADR-0004](../adr/0004-track-follows-openstreetmap.md)) already covers the core idea, and our `line-offset` slide is smoother than the zoom-14 cut. Six cheap 2D ideas are worth triage (Fits v1?), filed as #89–#94. 3D stays out of v1.

## 1. Per-track drawing, zoomed out and zoomed in

- **Geometry per track, not an offset.** railisland keeps OpenStreetMap's tracks as mapped: 5,637 OSM ways for TRA, HSR and the Alishan railway, from a 2026-09-07 snapshot ([`network.json`](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/blob/65a7adc729e7267f7fdc1ee901346a5598cb682c/rail-3d/physical/network.json)).
  - An offline solver gives each of 1,177 Trains a path through them, one chain of OSM ways per leg between Stations, with platform tracks, passing loops and holds where one Train waits for another ([`dispatch.json`](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/blob/65a7adc729e7267f7fdc1ee901346a5598cb682c/rail-3d/physical/dispatch.json); [physical README L3–L9](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/blob/65a7adc729e7267f7fdc1ee901346a5598cb682c/rail-3d/physical/README.md#L3-L9)).
  - At HSR Stations with through tracks, Trains that stop use the outer platform tracks and those that pass use the inner main line ([L12](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/blob/65a7adc729e7267f7fdc1ee901346a5598cb682c/rail-3d/physical/README.md#L12)). The author calls the assignment inferred, not official.
- **Those paths place the Trains at every zoom.** Where a TRA, HSR or Alishan Train has a plan, its position comes from its path; a Train without one falls back to its Line's single shape ([`index.html` L11586–L11598](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/blob/65a7adc729e7267f7fdc1ee901346a5598cb682c/index.html#L11586-L11598); [`client.js` L23–L48](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/blob/65a7adc729e7267f7fdc1ee901346a5598cb682c/rail-3d/physical/client.js#L23-L48); [`motion.js` L31–L72](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/blob/65a7adc729e7267f7fdc1ee901346a5598cb682c/rail-3d/physical/motion.js#L31-L72)).
  - Metro Lines have one traced track per direction, 36 in all, but only the 3D view uses them: it moves a metro Train's progress between Stations onto its direction's track ([`metro-motion.js` L5–L19](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/blob/65a7adc729e7267f7fdc1ee901346a5598cb682c/rail-3d/physical/metro-motion.js#L5-L19); [`rail-3d.js` L41–L91](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/blob/65a7adc729e7267f7fdc1ee901346a5598cb682c/rail-3d.js#L41-L91)).
- **Zoomed out (below 14).** Each Line is one schematic stroke, a casing and a colour in MapLibre `line` layers ([`index.html` L12981–L13116](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/blob/65a7adc729e7267f7fdc1ee901346a5598cb682c/index.html#L12981-L13116)). Trains are painted on a 2D canvas over the map: dots below zoom 10, number labels from 10 ([L13233–L13345](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/blob/65a7adc729e7267f7fdc1ee901346a5598cb682c/index.html#L13233-L13345)), metros from 11 ([L12425](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/blob/65a7adc729e7267f7fdc1ee901346a5598cb682c/index.html#L12425)).
  - A double track's two tracks are metres apart, so both ways land on one pixel. The drawing code has no running-side offset: the only thing that moves a marker off its position is the next item (read in the code, not seen on screen).
- **One computed offset, for Trains going the same way.** TRA only, number labels only, never the followed Train ([L11632–L11653](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/blob/65a7adc729e7267f7fdc1ee901346a5598cb682c/index.html#L11632-L11653), [L11795–L11855](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/blob/65a7adc729e7267f7fdc1ee901346a5598cb682c/index.html#L11795-L11855), [L13751–L13833](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/blob/65a7adc729e7267f7fdc1ee901346a5598cb682c/index.html#L13751-L13833)):
  - When two Trains on one Line going the same way come within 0.4 km and their labels would touch, the one standing at a Station moves off the line, to the right of its direction, by the pair's label widths. The passing one stays.
  - Otherwise the pair, or a longer string of Trains, alternate sides by half that each.
  - The shift runs along the normal of the Line's shape 50 m either side of the Train, sized to the label's width or height depending on that normal. It eases in at 18% a frame and holds until the pair is clear, so markers don't swap.
- **Direction shows by arrows, not sides.** An optional arrow on the label's leading edge, pointed from where the Train was 8 s earlier ([L13708](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/blob/65a7adc729e7267f7fdc1ee901346a5598cb682c/index.html#L13708), [L13834–L13844](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/blob/65a7adc729e7267f7fdc1ee901346a5598cb682c/index.html#L13834-L13844)). On dark or satellite basemaps, a tapering trail drawn from its last 44 seconds on the map's clock ([L13870–L13930](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/blob/65a7adc729e7267f7fdc1ee901346a5598cb682c/index.html#L13870-L13930)).
- **Zoomed in (14 and over).** For TRA, HSR and Alishan the strokes give way to every OSM track in view; a metro Line with both directions traced gives way to its two tracks ([`rail-3d.js` L81–L91](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/blob/65a7adc729e7267f7fdc1ee901346a5598cb682c/rail-3d.js#L81-L91); [`client.js` L31–L37](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/blob/65a7adc729e7267f7fdc1ee901346a5598cb682c/rail-3d/physical/client.js#L31-L37)).
  - A three.js layer draws them at rail height as thin lines of fixed screen width, 1.4–1.5 px with a casing ([`profile-lines.js`](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/blob/65a7adc729e7267f7fdc1ee901346a5598cb682c/rail-3d/integration/profile-lines.js#L1-L14)).
  - A Train drawn as a model loses its 2D marker ([`index.html` L13326](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/blob/65a7adc729e7267f7fdc1ee901346a5598cb682c/index.html#L13326)).
- **The switch is a hard cut.** The 3D layer gets no Trains below 13.8, unless a heading-up follow is locked ([`rail-3d.js` L121](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/blob/65a7adc729e7267f7fdc1ee901346a5598cb682c/rail-3d.js#L121)), and from 14 the swapped Lines are filtered out of the MapLibre layers ([`index.html` L13076–L13078](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/blob/65a7adc729e7267f7fdc1ee901346a5598cb682c/index.html#L13076-L13078)). Both are on or off, a filter and a visibility flag, with no crossfade and no offset easing. On the live site, over Taipei Main Station, 47 Line strokes left the filter between zoom 13.9 and 14.1, and 84 track paths appeared in the 3D layer.
- **Sideways shifting in 3D was tried and dropped.** An earlier version shifted whole Trains sideways to avoid overlaps; [`06dd003`](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/commit/06dd0034e4e0ec2c16cf26f92c84ba03b7881a43) (2026-09-08) removed it. Since then cars must follow the assigned track, and overlaps the solver leaves stay visible ([physical README L8–L9](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/blob/65a7adc729e7267f7fdc1ee901346a5598cb682c/rail-3d/physical/README.md#L8-L9)). The changelog dates separate tracks per direction on double track to 14 September, Taipei–Wanhua underground included ([`index.html` L5614](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/blob/65a7adc729e7267f7fdc1ee901346a5598cb682c/index.html#L5614)).

## 2. Train models at scale

- **Renderer: three.js r170 in MapLibre 5.9.0 custom layers.** No deck.gl and no glTF; fill-extrusion is used only for buildings. One three.js renderer shares MapLibre's canvas and GL context and draws with the map's camera, from a fixed anchor in metres ([`map3d.js` L45–L46](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/blob/65a7adc729e7267f7fdc1ee901346a5598cb682c/rail-3d/integration/map3d.js#L45-L46), [L405–L436](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/blob/65a7adc729e7267f7fdc1ee901346a5598cb682c/rail-3d/integration/map3d.js#L405-L436); [3D README L6](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/blob/65a7adc729e7267f7fdc1ee901346a5598cb682c/rail-3d/README.md#L6)). There are four custom layers: the main one, a pass for tunnel entrances, a pass under see-through buildings, and a see-through pass for everything underground. Stations are a fifth.
- **Models: made in Blender, shipped as raw vertex buffers.** 62 models from 110 meshes, mostly a head and a middle mesh per type, with a median of about 24,000 vertices: position, normal, colour and gloss per vertex, no textures ([`manifest.json`](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/blob/65a7adc729e7267f7fdc1ee901346a5598cb682c/rail-3d/assets/blender-map-v1/manifest.json); [`wenhu.js` L15–L21](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/blob/65a7adc729e7267f7fdc1ee901346a5598cb682c/rail-3d/assets/wenhu-v1/wenhu.js#L15-L21)). Each file is checked by length and SHA-256 as it loads ([`map3d.js` L252–L260](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/blob/65a7adc729e7267f7fdc1ee901346a5598cb682c/rail-3d/integration/map3d.js#L252-L260)). They're served uncompressed: 0.96 MB for an HSR 700T head car, 1.26 MB for its middle car.
- **Coupled cars along curves:**
  - A table gives each Train type's cars in metres, such as HSR's 700T as 12 cars, 27 m at the ends and 25 m between. It says whether the count is a standard or an estimate; the follow card shows which, and a three-car version is a user option ([`formations.js` L8–L86](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/blob/65a7adc729e7267f7fdc1ee901346a5598cb682c/rail-3d/integration/formations.js#L8-L86); [`FORMATIONS.md`](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/blob/65a7adc729e7267f7fdc1ee901346a5598cb682c/rail-3d/integration/FORMATIONS.md)).
  - A Train is assembled from the head mesh, the middle mesh repeated and the head mesh turned round. Each car body is 0.16 m short at each coupled end, so bodies stand about 0.32 m apart ([L90–L104](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/blob/65a7adc729e7267f7fdc1ee901346a5598cb682c/rail-3d/integration/formations.js#L90-L104)).
  - Each car goes at its own distance along the Train's track. It's turned to the chord a few metres either side (at most 8) and tilted by the rail heights there, so cars bend round curves and take turnouts one after another. If the Train's front or back would run off the known track, no model is drawn and the marker stays ([`train-path.js` L38–L43](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/blob/65a7adc729e7267f7fdc1ee901346a5598cb682c/rail-3d/integration/train-path.js#L38-L43); [`map3d.js` L309–L345](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/blob/65a7adc729e7267f7fdc1ee901346a5598cb682c/rail-3d/integration/map3d.js#L309-L345)).
- **Readable size.** Below zoom 19 cars are widened, at most twice, to be about 4 px wide, and the widening fades out from 17 to 19. Length, gaps and height stay true ([`readability.js` L3–L9](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/blob/65a7adc729e7267f7fdc1ee901346a5598cb682c/rail-3d/integration/readability.js#L3-L9)).
- **Rails and viaducts are generated.** One merged, vertex-coloured mesh is rebuilt as the view moves ([`map3d.js` L187–L251](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/blob/65a7adc729e7267f7fdc1ee901346a5598cb682c/rail-3d/integration/map3d.js#L187-L251); [`rail-structures.js` L1–L30](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/blob/65a7adc729e7267f7fdc1ee901346a5598cb682c/rail-3d/integration/rail-structures.js#L1-L30), [L112–L129](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/blob/65a7adc729e7267f7fdc1ee901346a5598cb682c/rail-3d/integration/rail-structures.js#L112-L129), [L203–L217](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/blob/65a7adc729e7267f7fdc1ee901346a5598cb682c/rail-3d/integration/rail-structures.js#L203-L217)):
  - Each track gets a ballast bed or a box-girder deck; track 6 m or more above the ground is drawn as viaduct.
  - Piers stand on a 32 m grid that lines up across parallel tracks, and parapets only where no track runs beside.
  - Rails appear from zoom 17 and sleepers from 18.5 ([`map3d.js` L144–L145](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/blob/65a7adc729e7267f7fdc1ee901346a5598cb682c/rail-3d/integration/map3d.js#L144-L145)).
- **Heights are estimates.** They come from OSM's bridge and tunnel tags, checked against the national mapping centre's bridge and tunnel data and a terrain model, with a grade limit per Network. The data file says they're not engineering levels ([`level-profiles.json`](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/blob/65a7adc729e7267f7fdc1ee901346a5598cb682c/rail-3d/physical/level-profiles.json), its `basis`; [`structure-kind.js`](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/blob/65a7adc729e7267f7fdc1ee901346a5598cb682c/rail-3d/physical/structure-kind.js#L1-L18)). The author found 13 viaducts mapped as tunnels in OSM, 6 of them on HSR ([#57](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/issues/57)).
- **Portals.** The build lists 686 portal positions and bearings. From zoom 17, those in view are merged across adjacent tracks and drawn with an arched face, a bore that darkens inwards and wing walls ([`tunnel-portals.js`](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/blob/65a7adc729e7267f7fdc1ee901346a5598cb682c/rail-3d/integration/tunnel-portals.js#L1-L25); [`rail-structures.js` L131–L196](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/blob/65a7adc729e7267f7fdc1ee901346a5598cb682c/rail-3d/integration/rail-structures.js#L131-L196)). With terrain on, a shader rebuilds each bore's depth, since the terrain has no holes ([`tunnel-apertures.js`](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/blob/65a7adc729e7267f7fdc1ee901346a5598cb682c/rail-3d/integration/tunnel-apertures.js#L1-L27)).
- **Underground is a see-through pass.** Underground track is drawn at 32% opacity with no bed or rails, in a pass that clears depth so it shows through the ground; with terrain on, mountain tunnels are left for the hill to hide. Underground Trains are drawn at 42% in the default see-through mode ([`profile-lines.js` L8](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/blob/65a7adc729e7267f7fdc1ee901346a5598cb682c/rail-3d/integration/profile-lines.js#L8); [`map3d.js` L59–L60](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/blob/65a7adc729e7267f7fdc1ee901346a5598cb682c/rail-3d/integration/map3d.js#L59-L60), [L146–L185](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/blob/65a7adc729e7267f7fdc1ee901346a5598cb682c/rail-3d/integration/map3d.js#L146-L185), [L427–L436](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/blob/65a7adc729e7267f7fdc1ee901346a5598cb682c/rail-3d/integration/map3d.js#L427-L436)). So zoomed in, every track is a thin line: above ground it sits on a 3D bed, below ground it's faint.
- **Glow.** Each model gets two soft screen-space ellipses per car in its Line's colour, warmer at night or in dark mode. They're batched into two buffers each frame, above and below ground, and depth-tested so buildings cover them ([`train-halo.js`](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/blob/65a7adc729e7267f7fdc1ee901346a5598cb682c/rail-3d/integration/train-halo.js#L6-L75); [`train-halo-style.js` L1–L31](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/blob/65a7adc729e7267f7fdc1ee901346a5598cb682c/rail-3d/integration/train-halo-style.js#L1-L31)). It's a setting, on by default ([`index.html` L7797–L7799](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/blob/65a7adc729e7267f7fdc1ee901346a5598cb682c/index.html#L7797-L7799)). With it on, every 2D marker gets a soft halo in its Line's colour too, on any basemap ([L7812–L7817](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/blob/65a7adc729e7267f7fdc1ee901346a5598cb682c/index.html#L7812-L7817)). Dark and satellite basemaps add a neon glow under TRA and HSR markers ([L13851–L13867](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/blob/65a7adc729e7267f7fdc1ee901346a5598cb682c/index.html#L13851-L13867)).
- **Limits in the code:**
  - Models from zoom 14 only, for Trains in view or followed, all of them by default ([`map3d.js` L295–L300](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/blob/65a7adc729e7267f7fdc1ee901346a5598cb682c/rail-3d/integration/map3d.js#L295-L300); [`rail-3d.js` L22–L24](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/blob/65a7adc729e7267f7fdc1ee901346a5598cb682c/rail-3d.js#L22-L24)). Cars aren't instanced: each car is its own mesh.
  - At most 18 idle meshes stay cached ([`map3d.js` L352–L353](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/blob/65a7adc729e7267f7fdc1ee901346a5598cb682c/rail-3d/integration/map3d.js#L352-L353)).
  - Headlights light the ground from zoom 16 at night, for 8 Trains on screens under 768 px wide and 24 otherwise ([L309–L336](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/blob/65a7adc729e7267f7fdc1ee901346a5598cb682c/rail-3d/integration/map3d.js#L309-L336)).
  - Underground track is sampled every 20 m instead of 5 m, after a rebuild at Taipei Main Station took the author 559 ms on a 6× slowed desktop ([L219–L222](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/blob/65a7adc729e7267f7fdc1ee901346a5598cb682c/rail-3d/integration/map3d.js#L219-L222)).
- **Power-save mode** ([`index.html` L17312–L17320](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/blob/65a7adc729e7267f7fdc1ee901346a5598cb682c/index.html#L17312-L17320), [L17372–L17395](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/blob/65a7adc729e7267f7fdc1ee901346a5598cb682c/index.html#L17372-L17395), [L37016–L37020](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/blob/65a7adc729e7267f7fdc1ee901346a5598cb682c/index.html#L37016-L37020), [L8239](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/blob/65a7adc729e7267f7fdc1ee901346a5598cb682c/index.html#L8239)):
  - On by default on screens up to 640 px wide, it caps the 2D redraws, the camera and the 3D updates at about 30 a second, except for 1.5 s after a touch.

  - Nothing is drawn in a hidden tab, and the 2D canvas stops at 2× pixel ratio.

### Measured

- **How:**
  - Chromium 153 through Playwright on an Apple M4 Pro, GPU through ANGLE Metal; viewport 390 × 844 CSS px at pixel ratio 1; 120 Hz.
  - CPU slowed 4× with CDP's `Emulation.setCPUThrottlingRate`; the GPU wasn't.
  - Frame rate: `requestAnimationFrame` callbacks counted over 12–15 s per view. Draw calls: WebGL's draw functions wrapped by an init script. Also railisland's own counters: its 2D redraws and the 3D layer's stats.
  - Bytes: the compressed sizes Chrome reported, with the cache disabled and no service worker.
  - Power-save was on, the default at that width, so railisland redrew about 27 times a second where it could keep up.
- **A throttled desktop is not a phone.** The GPU ran at full speed, a phone has two to three times the pixel ratio (four to nine times the pixels), and phones slow down when hot. These numbers compare views; they don't predict a phone's frame rate.
- **Load:** a cold load of the default whole-island view moved 15.45 MB in 203 requests over 35 s.
  - 11.3 MB of it was track and height data. The track module's six files, 10.4 MB, load at start, because the 2D markers take their positions from it too: display profiles 4.80 MB, levels 3.23, metro profiles 0.95, network 0.76, dispatch 0.37, metro network 0.26 ([`rail-3d.js` L15](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/blob/65a7adc729e7267f7fdc1ee901346a5598cb682c/rail-3d.js#L15); [`client.js` L24–L28](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/blob/65a7adc729e7267f7fdc1ee901346a5598cb682c/rail-3d/physical/client.js#L24-L28)).
  - The 3D layer, on by default, adds its own heights (0.96 MB) and three.js (0.28 MB) at start. MapLibre is 0.26 MB.
  - Zooming into Taipei Main Station fetched 5.84 MB more in 35 s, mostly six car meshes of 0.92–0.96 MB.
  - Terrain, off by default except on the landscape basemap, is 116 MB, fetched by range only when on ([`terrain/manifest.json`](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/blob/65a7adc729e7267f7fdc1ee901346a5598cb682c/rail-3d/terrain/manifest.json)).

## 3. Against our map

- **We already have the core idea.** Our daily build traces each way on its own track of a double track, on the Network's running side ([ADR-0004](../adr/0004-track-follows-openstreetmap.md), #25), so zoomed in our Trains already run on their own tracks. railisland also picks platform tracks and passing loops with an offline solver. We have no platforms to give one, though FGC's trip updates name them, such as PC1 ([live-data-sources.md](live-data-sources.md)).
- **Our zoom handling is smoother.** Zoomed out, our Lines that share track are drawn a line width apart, and they slide back onto the rails by zoom 15 (`APART` in [`main.ts`](https://github.com/gariasf/viapeninsula/blob/75c940afab86ae5ee65898502535fe15be9d5d36/src/web/main.ts#L22-L28)). Our markers sit half a line width to their Network's running side there, so opposite ways show apart where railisland's overlap (`trains()`, [L366–L396](https://github.com/gariasf/viapeninsula/blob/75c940afab86ae5ee65898502535fe15be9d5d36/src/web/main.ts#L366-L396)). railisland cuts from strokes to tracks at 14.
- **What railisland has in 2D that we don't:** markers of Trains going the same way kept apart, direction arrows, a glow, a count of Trains running, and capped idle redraws.
- **Our loop runs flat out.** We call `setData` on the Trains' GeoJSON source on every animation frame, 60 to 120 times a second, and MapLibre redraws each time (the `move()` loop in [`main.ts`](https://github.com/gariasf/viapeninsula/blob/75c940afab86ae5ee65898502535fe15be9d5d36/src/web/main.ts#L294-L313)). railisland redraws its canvas about 30 times a second when idle, and leaves MapLibre idle zoomed out.

**Against our map:**

- railisland's fill says the kind of service. Ours says whether a Train is Live, filled with its Line's colour, or Scheduled, ringed with it (spec story 5). So for us the kind of service has to go in the shape, and the fill stays Live or Scheduled.
- Our markers are dots at every zoom, and our Line names are only along the strokes. Our Lines have no casing and widen with the zoom (`WIDTH`, `APART`), and every Station is the same white circle ringed in #444, named from zoom 12, under the Station and over the Trains there.

**What #90 takes from it:**

- **A pill with the Line's name.** It's what riders read off platforms and signs, R5 and R50 are different services, and only Renfe publishes Train numbers, which riders hardly use. Our Line names fit a pill, at 2 to 4 characters from R5 to L10N. The Montserrat rack and the two funiculars go by name rather than code, and keep their codes, MM, FV and FM, for now.
- **Shape by kind of service:** rounded for commuter and suburban Lines (R1–R8, RG1, RT1 and RT2, RL3 and RL4, FGC's S and L Lines), pointed at both ends for regional ones (R11–R17, and FGC's R5, R50, R6, R60, RL1 and RL2), and a rounded square for the Metro, TRAM, the rack and the funiculars.
- **Live filled, Scheduled white**, ringed and lettered in the Line's colour.
- **The arrow outside the pill's leading edge**, from the engine's heading along the Trip's shape at the Train's place, as #90's triage decided, rather than from where it was 8 s before.
- **A prototype first**, on branch `90-pills-prototype`, with three variants on the map: A, dots and then pills from zoom 12; B, pills at every zoom and no names along the Lines; C, today's dot with the Line's name beside it and the arrow on its rim. - **The ideas for Lines and Stations are filed for triage:** a casing (#118), Stations by importance (#119), names beside the track (#120), and hiding a town's name beside its Station's (#121).

## Fits v1?

Filed as needs-triage issues, #89–#94. Each is 2D, uses data the map already has, and changes only the web app. Following a Train is already in v1 (#16).

- **Nudge a Train standing at a Station aside while another on its track passes** (#89). We know no platforms, so both sit on one point; railisland moves the standing one's marker aside and eases it back once they part.
- **Show which way each Train runs with a small arrow on its marker** (#90). Zoomed in, each way has its own track, but a marker alone doesn't say which way it's going. On 2026-09-27 it became railisland's pill: each Train drawn with its Line's name, shaped by its kind of service, with the arrow outside the pill (section 4).
- **Put a soft halo in the Line's colour under each marker** (#91). railisland puts one under every 2D marker by default. Check that Live (filled) and Scheduled (ringed) still read apart.
- **Show by the legend how many Trains are running, and how many are Live** (#92). railisland counts Trains running and Lines on timetable estimates; ours would show the Live and Scheduled split ([ADR-0002](../adr/0002-timetable-drives-motion.md)).
- **Move the markers about 30 times a second while nobody touches the map** (#93). It isn't the power-save mode the spec puts later: railisland caps idle redraws with its power-save off too. It serves the spec's smooth-on-a-phone story; measure ours first.
- **Add "Follow a random Train" to #16's follow** (#94). One button that picks a Train on the map and follows it. No v1 story asks for it, but it's a small addition to #16.

**Not v1**, each with its cheapest 2D form:

- **Faint track underground:** carry OSM's tunnel tags through the trace and draw those stretches fainter. It changes the bundle's format, as #25 did, and the tags need a check first: railisland found viaducts mapped as tunnels ([#57](https://github.com/siriushsu/taiwan-rail-live/issues/57)).
- **Trains drawn to length zoomed in**, the 2D stand-in for Unit models: a thick line from the Train's head back along its track, one piece per Unit. It needs each Train's Units and their lengths, which only FGC's Geotren (the Unit series) and TRAM (the Unit) publish ([live-data-sources.md](live-data-sources.md)). Elsewhere they'd be estimates.
- **3D models, rails, viaducts and portals:** 3D is out of scope. They take three.js in custom layers, a mesh per car, and about 11 MB of track and height data before any of it shows.
- **Platform tracks and passing loops:** they need an offline solver like railisland's, and platform data we mostly lack.
- **Time slider and passport:** the spec puts time travel, playback speeds and the passport "later".
